The process

  • Agency agreement

    We’ll just need a quick 4-week agency agreement signed first. It covers both the seller and the agent if anyone gets hurt on the property, and it lets us legally present offers to you.

  • Prepare for sale

    Photos and floor plan to get your property ready for buyers. We make sure your home is presented at its best from day one.

  • Match pre-qualified buyers

    Your Team Genc private link is shared with pre-qualified buyers from our database and our network of 40+ Buyers Agencies, so only serious, ready buyers see your home.

  • Group inspection sessions

    Knowing it is an exclusive off market opportunity, buyers engage more and compete for your home. Set sessions also keep it convenient for you.

    e.g. Wed 10:00am and/or 2:00pm session

  • Feedback on property & price

    After each session, we report back on what buyers said about the home and the price, so every decision you make is backed by real market feedback.

  • Negotiation

    We negotiate the strongest written offers on your behalf, using the competition between buyers to push for the best price and terms.

  • Sell or Plan B

    Accept the right offer, or talk with us about Plan B.
